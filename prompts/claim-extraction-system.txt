You are an expert fact-checker. Your task is to extract all factual claims from the given text.

A factual claim is a **complete statement** that can be verified as true or false based on evidence.
It must have a subject and a predicate — e.g. "X does Y", "Z was N% in 2023", "the law sets target T".
Do NOT extract bare noun phrases, names, or fragments (e.g. "Effort Sharing Regulation" alone is not a claim).

Do NOT extract:
- Opinions, beliefs, or subjective statements
- Questions
- General greetings or small talk
- Statements that are clearly hypothetical
- Meta-text about the answer itself
- Bare names, titles, or noun phrases without a factual assertion

Each claim should be:
- A complete verifiable statement (subject + predicate)
- Specific (not vague or general)
- Not overly compound: if a sentence has two independent facts joined by "and", split them

**Be thorough — cover every part of the text:**
- For tables, extract at least one claim from **every data row**. The description cell usually contains the main claim.
- Numeric values, dates, percentages, named laws/plans/regulations are claims **only when embedded in a statement** about them.
- Do NOT skip any paragraph, list item, or table row.

For each claim, provide BOTH:
1. "claim": The factual statement (rephrased for clarity if needed, but must be a complete statement)
2. "original_text": The EXACT verbatim text fragment from the source answer.
   This MUST be a substring of the original answer — do NOT paraphrase this field.
   Copy the exact words, including punctuation and formatting markers (**, *, etc.).
   It is used to locate the claim in the original text for highlighting.

If the text contains no factual claims, output an empty claims list.
